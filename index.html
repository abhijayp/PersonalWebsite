About Me

I’m a student passionate about computer science, AI, and building real-world projects that make learning and ideas more accessible. I started coding at a young age and have been developing my skills through Python, web development, and hands-on projects that combine creativity with problem-solving. I’m a Public Forum debater with competitive tournament experience, and I enjoy breaking down arguments, thinking critically, and improving how I communicate complex ideas. I’m also the founder of KidsWhoTeach.org, where I work on peer-to-peer learning initiatives, and I’ve been involved in other community-focused projects like iSpyWithMyLittleEye.org. Outside of academics and tech, I’m really interested in basketball, especially game strategy and analysis, and I enjoy solving challenges like Rubik’s cubes and logic puzzles. I’m always looking to improve as a builder, speaker, and thinker, with a focus on turning ideas into projects that actually have impact.
